## When Does the Sephora Black Friday Sale Actually Start?

Sephora starts its holiday savings well before Black Friday itself. Sephora Canada has confirmed its Fall Savings Event 2026 runs **October 30 through November 9**, opening first for Rouge members and rolling out to lower tiers a few days later — and the US event historically follows the same tiered structure, with TopCashback's own Sephora page listing the same 10/15/20% tier discounts. Sephora's dedicated Black Friday page is already live on its US site, promising skincare, makeup and gift sets "on-budget," and pointing shoppers to the Sephora app for exclusive holiday deals and product previews.

The pattern from recent years is worth understanding, because it changes what you buy when. Instead of discounting the whole store at once, Sephora's Black Friday period works on daily drops: specific brands get deep, limited-time discounts each day during Cyber Week. The tiered Savings Event that runs before it is the opposite — a sitewide percentage off that you can use on anything, multiple times.

So the practical calendar looks like this:

- **Late October:** Savings Event opens for Rouge (20% off everything), plus 30% off Sephora Collection for all tiers from October 30
- **Early November:** VIB (15%) and Insider (10%) get their windows, through November 9 in Canada
- **Week of Thanksgiving:** the Black Friday daily drops begin, with select brands at up to 50% off
- **Cyber Monday and after:** gift sets and holiday kits get marked down, with clearance discounts after Christmas

One caution: exact US dates for this year haven't been published in the sources available at the time of writing, so treat the confirmed Canadian schedule as a strong preview rather than a guarantee, and check the dates inside your Beauty Insider account before building a basket.
